## Why there are no stock photos

Stock photography was researched on Unsplash. The salon photos found there were **Unsplash+** images (a separate, subscription license), so they were not used. No free-license photo was verified, so, per the project rules, the page uses generated visuals instead of unverified external images.

To add photos later, use real photos of the salon or verified free-license images, then record each one here with its direct URL, original page, creator, license and required attribution.
